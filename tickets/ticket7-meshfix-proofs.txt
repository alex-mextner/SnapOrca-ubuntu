== Criterion 1: the failing assertion and its cause, as recorded in the commit message of f76fe9254b (the original Catch2 failure output was not kept) ==
meshfix: rejoin pinched vertices after hole filling, separate the rest along one axis

A vertex where several holes touch was split into one copy per fan, the holes were
filled per loop, and the copies were nudged apart along their own normals. Around a
sphere pole this made the nudged copies' faces cross, so the exact CGAL
does_self_intersect() check failed on "CGAL repair closes many small holes"
(580 intersecting pairs, all between faces made of nudged pole copies).

rejoin_pinched_vertices() merges the copies back where the faces then form one closed
fan, dropping the collapsed faces; the pole case needs no nudge at all (0 pairs, volume
exact). Copies that must stay apart move along their common normal axis in growing
steps (golden-angle sideways offset so a triangle of copies keeps an area) and keep
the old per-normal nudge when the normals cancel (parts touching from opposite sides).
